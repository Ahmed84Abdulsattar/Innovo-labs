# Innovo Labs — Innovation Management Platform

## Product Purpose
A web application for tracking, evaluating, and matching construction-tech startups to internal project needs across Innovo Group's business units: Build, MEP, Development, Infrastructure, and Digital Innovation.

## Users
- **Innovation Admins** — curate the startup database, manage the pipeline, assess and onboard startups
- **Business Unit Managers** — browse startups relevant to their department, rate and leave feedback
- **Project Teams** — use the Innovation Concierge to find startups matched to their specific project context
- **Executives** — review the initiatives and collaboration pipeline across the portfolio

## Core Flows
1. **Startup pipeline** — add, assess, pilot, and onboard startups through a collaboration lifecycle (Identified → Assessed → Business Review → Pilot → Onboarded)
2. **Innovation Concierge** — guided questionnaire matching startups by stakeholder role, project sector, location, complexity, and strategic goals
3. **Initiatives** — track startup deployments by business unit and department
4. **Ideas** — collect and triage employee problem statements, with a value tracker and status timeline

## Brand & Tone
Professional, data-confident, precise. Serves construction industry professionals who value clarity over decoration. Dark slate primary (#122023) with teal accent (#0f9790). Light surface mode: white cards on a cool gray (#f7f9fa) background. Clean and structured — not flashy.
